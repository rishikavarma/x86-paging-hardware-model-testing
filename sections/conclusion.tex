\section{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

Efficiently scaling to longer traces, additional cores, and incorporating more complex microarchitectural events is a work in progress and left for future work.


% TODO: Summarise contributions and outline future work.
In this paper, we presented a proof-of-concept model validation strategy for the x86 paging hardware. We proposed a reduction argument to reduce the testing space and a method to analyze the observed execution traces to identify if they are allowed by the model through synthesis. These ideas can be extended to a more comprehensive model including robust proofs for the reduction arguments which we leave for future work.
