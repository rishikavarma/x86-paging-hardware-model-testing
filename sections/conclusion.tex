\section{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

We presented a proof-of-concept validation strategy for a formal hardware model against its real-world counterpart. 
We proposed a reduction argument to reduce the state space and a trace-validation technique based on software synthesis.
Our evaluation shows that this can be a viable approach in validating formal hardware specifications.

While our work shows promising early results, this is just one step towards a full fidelity model, 
including more complex micro-architectural interactions. While we provided informal arguments for our model-reduction approach targeted at the x86 MMU, a formal proof would strengthen the case. This aspect of our methodology would vary most across different hardware models and could pose scalability challenges if the model cannot be reduced efficiently.
We leave these aspects, along with improving our prototype, for future work.
